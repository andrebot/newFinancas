Delete Budget

DELETE /households/:householdId/budgets/:budgetId — FR-4.10/OQ-40. Hard delete of the live definition only — unlike every other archive/delete decision in this project, past Budget Periods are unaffected and survive on their own.

  • Corrected (OQ-176): deleting is a data.delete on the Budget, authorized like any write — a shared Budget needs Owner/Admin or the Member who created it; a personal one is its owner's alone. The earlier diagram had no AuthorizationUtility edge.
  • Deleting also frees every Category/Subcategory this Budget had claimed, making them available to a new Budget in the same scope immediately. Deleting it again is 404: nothing is left.
