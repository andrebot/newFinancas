Record Market Value

POST /households/:householdId/holdings/:holdingId/valuation-snapshots — FR-5.2/OQ-56. Manual Valuation Snapshot of a market-priced Holding (stock, FII, fund, other).

sequenceDiagram
    actor User
    participant API as Hono route
    participant AM as AccountManager
    participant AU as AuthorizationUtility
    participant IHA as InvestmentHoldingAccessor
    participant AA as AccountAccessor
    participant VU as ValidationUtility
    participant VSA as ValuationSnapshotAccessor
    participant LOG as LoggingUtility

    User->>API: POST /households/:householdId/holdings/:holdingId/valuation-snapshots {value, asOf}
    API->>AM: recordMarketValue(context, householdId, holdingId, input)
    AM->>IHA: findById(householdId, holdingId)
    IHA-->>AM: holding (or none)
    AM->>AA: findById(householdId, holding.accountId)
    AA-->>AM: its Account
    AM->>AU: authorize(actor, householdId, {data.edit, account.visibility, account.ownerUserId})
    alt not a member
        AM-->>API: 404 household.not_found
    else missing, or in someone else's personal Account
        AM-->>API: 404 holding.not_found
    else role may not maintain (a Member may on a shared Account they created)
        AM-->>API: 403 auth.forbidden
    else allowed
        alt Holding archived
            AM-->>API: 409 holding.archived
        else fixed-term (OQ-51)
            AM-->>API: 409 holding.not_market_priced
        else market-priced
            AM->>VU: checkFields({asOf: dateWithin(max: today)})
            alt asOf after today
                AM-->>API: 422 validation.failed {asOf: date.max}
            else
                AM->>VSA: insertManual(holdingId, {valuation, asOfDate})
                VSA-->>AM: snapshot
                AM-->>LOG: audit RecordMarketValue
                API-->>User: 201 Created {snapshot}
            end
        end
    end
  • Fixed-term Holdings have no snapshots (value = cost basis until redemption, OQ-51). The value is zero or more (NonNegativeMoney).
  • As built (OQ-178): access is the Holding's Account's (data.edit for all three writes); an archived Holding takes no new value; a date after today is refused on the field (date.max), as everywhere else (OQ-165).
  • Net worth and goal progress read “latest snapshot at or before month end” on demand — nothing to recompute here.
  • See FR-5.2, OQ-51, OQ-56, OQ-83, OQ-178.
