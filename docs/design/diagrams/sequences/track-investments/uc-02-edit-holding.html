Edit Holding Goal Allocations

GET /households/:householdId/holdings/:holdingId/allocations, PATCH /households/:householdId/holdings/:holdingId — FR-8.2/8.3/OQ-48. Allocations only — position is transaction-only, market value has its own use cases.

            alt a Goal named twice

                AM-->>API: 404 goal.not_found
            else a completed Goal's share grown or added (FR-8.9)

                alt total would exceed 100%
                    GA-->>AM: allocation_exceeds_100 {current, requested}
                    AM-->>API: 409 goal.allocation_exceeds_100 {current, requested}
                else Holding or Goal gone meanwhile
                    GA-->>AM: holding_not_found / goal_not_found
                    AM-->>API: 404 holding.not_found / goal.not_found
                else replaced

                    AM-->>LOG: audit EditHoldingAllocations
                    API-->>User: 200 OK {data: the split as written}

  • Direction of control (OQ-48): allocations are written from the Holding, never from the Goal.
  • Replace-all over what the caller can see (OQ-178): a shared Holding may fund someone else's personal Goal; the editor never sees that share, so the Manager carries it over unchanged and it still counts toward the 100% — the 409's totals include it without naming it.
  • The ≤ 100% check is a single comparison in GoalAccessor, made under the Holding's row lock.
  • Business rules are the Manager's (OQ-168): a Goal the actor can't see is “not found”, never revealed; a completed Goal's share may stay unchanged or be removed, never grow or be added.
  • Access (OQ-178): a Holding takes its Account's visibility and owner; an archived Holding is closed to edits.
  • See FR-8.2, FR-8.3, OQ-48, OQ-168, OQ-178.
