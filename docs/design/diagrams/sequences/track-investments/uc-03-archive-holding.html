Archive Matured Holding

POST /households/:householdId/holdings/:holdingId/archive — FR-5.8/OQ-43/OQ-72. Reached from the “past due — archive?” notification. One-way.

sequenceDiagram
    actor User
    participant API as Hono route
    participant AM as AccountManager
    participant AU as AuthorizationUtility
    participant IHA as InvestmentHoldingAccessor
    participant AA as AccountAccessor
    participant LOG as LoggingUtility

    User->>API: POST /households/:householdId/holdings/:holdingId/archive
    API->>AM: archiveHolding(context, householdId, holdingId)
    AM->>IHA: findById(householdId, holdingId)
    IHA-->>AM: holding (or none)
    AM->>AA: findById(householdId, holding.accountId)
    AA-->>AM: its Account
    AM->>AU: authorize(actor, householdId, {data.delete, account.visibility, account.ownerUserId})
    alt not a member
        AM-->>API: 404 household.not_found
    else missing, or in someone else's personal Account
        AM-->>API: 404 holding.not_found
    else role may not archive (a Member may on a shared Account they created)
        AM-->>API: 403 auth.forbidden
    else allowed
        alt already archived
            AM-->>API: nothing to write, unaudited
            API-->>User: 204 No Content
        else open
            AM->>IHA: listSchedule([holdingId])
            IHA-->>AM: the schedule
            alt not fixed-term, no position, or not past its redemption date
                AM-->>API: 409 holding.not_matured
            else matured
                AM->>IHA: archive(holdingId, by = user)
                Note over IHA: one-way — a user-made archive is never undone
automatically (contrast OQ-80)
                alt archived meanwhile
                    IHA-->>AM: false
                    API-->>User: 204 No Content (unaudited)
                else archived now
                    IHA-->>AM: true
                    AM-->>LOG: audit ArchiveHolding
                    API-->>User: 204 No Content
                end
            end
        end
    end
    Note over User,API: frontend then calls DELETE /notifications/:notificationId
(Insights UC-6) to clear the notice
  • Only a matured Holding (OQ-178): fixed-term, still holding a position, past its due date — the redemption entry's date, the same rule findMaturedWithPosition uses. Every other archive happens automatically in Record Transaction when a trade zeroes the Holding.
  • Archiving is data.delete on the Holding's Account, like archiving an Account.
  • See FR-5.8, OQ-43, OQ-72, OQ-80, OQ-178.
