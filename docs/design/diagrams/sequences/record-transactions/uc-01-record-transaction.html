Record Transaction

POST /households/:householdId/transactions — FR-3.1/OQ-39. One unified entry point for both Account and Card Transactions — target narrows which kinds are valid, itself data-driven. The investment-buy-against-a-fixed-term-instrument sub-case is one of the two widest-fan-out flows in the whole system.

    User->>API: POST /households/:householdId/transactions {accountId | creditCardId, kind, date, amount, currency, categoryId?, description, holdingId? | assetType + ticker?, quantity?, rate?, dueDate?, interestPayment?, incentivised?, allocations?}
    API->>TM: record(actor, input)
    Note over TM: loads the target (Account or card + its Account — a bill payment's card may sit on any Account
the actor may record on, open and in the payment's currency, checked after the payer, OQ-219) and, if given, the Category —
an archived Account or a newly used archived Category is refused (OQ-169, OQ-201)
    TM->>AZ: canRecord(actor, targetId)? — creating on its Account: any Member on a shared one (OQ-223)
    alt not authorized
        AZ-->>TM: denied
        TM-->>API: Forbidden
        API-->>User: 403 auth.forbidden
    else authorized, target is a Credit Card
        AZ-->>TM: allowed
        alt kind not in {charge, refund}
            TM-->>API: ValidationError (FR-3.1 — a Credit Card target only offers charge/refund)
            API-->>User: 422 transaction.kind_not_allowed_for_target
        else charge or refund
            TM->>CCA: applyMovement(cardId, {signedAmount, currency, date})
            Note over CCA: positive for a charge, negative for a refund (the Manager signs it),
lands in the billing cycle the date falls in (OQ-170)
            CCA-->>TM: outstanding balance updated
            TM->>BA: accrueIfClaimed({accountScope, currency, categoryId, subcategoryId, ±amount, date})
            BA-->>TM: spend updated (no-op if unclaimed, a refund subtracts — OQ-201)
            TM->>CTA: insert({creditCardId, kind, date, amount, currency, categoryId, subcategoryId, description, createdByUserId})
            alt the card or the recorder was deleted meanwhile (a Category can't be — FR-10.3)
                CTA-->>TM: {ok: false, reason: reference_not_found}
                Note over TM: refuse — the unit of work rolls the card movement and accrual back (OQ-203)
                TM-->>API: Conflict
                API-->>User: 409 transaction.changed (retry gets the precise 404)
            else recorded
                CTA-->>TM: {ok: true, transaction}
                TM-->>LOG: logActivity(correlationId, actor, "RecordTransaction")
                TM-->>LOG: recordAudit(actor, "Transaction", transactionId)
                TM-->>API: transaction
                API-->>User: 201 Created {transaction}
            end
        end
    else authorized, target is an Account
        AZ-->>TM: allowed
        Note over TM: effect = EFFECT_BY_KIND[kind] — inline reference-data lookup
        alt currency does not match the target account's currency
            TM-->>API: ValidationError (FR-2.2 — no cross-currency movements)
            API-->>User: 422 transaction.currency_mismatch
        else investment kind on a credit-card-only account
            TM-->>API: ValidationError (OQ-76 — investments live in checking, savings or investment accounts)
            API-->>User: 422 transaction.investment_not_allowed_on_account
        else any other kind on a credit-card-only account, a bill payment included
            TM-->>API: ValidationError (OQ-219 — its cards' bills are paid from another account)
            API-->>User: 422 transaction.kind_not_allowed_for_target
        else fixed-term buy: dueDate not after date, a spread on a rate not CDI/Selic,
or a rate missing its type or value (OQ-207)
            TM-->>API: ValidationError (OQ-205 — the Engine is never called)
            API-->>User: 422 validation.failed {dueDate | rate.spread | rate.type | rate.value}
        else a transfer_sent with toAccountId — between own accounts (OQ-222)
            TM->>AA: findById(toAccountId) — authorized and gated like the From account
            alt the same account, a receivedAmount missing across currencies or given within one
                TM-->>API: ValidationError
                API-->>User: 422 validation.failed {toAccountId | receivedAmount}
            else both accounts take it
                TM->>ATA: insertTransfer({sent, received}) — both effect = transfer, linked by transfer_id
                TM->>AA: applyMovement(fromAccountId, −amount)
                TM->>AA: applyMovement(toAccountId, +receivedAmount or amount)
                Note over TM: no category, no Budget accrual — neither spending nor income
            end
        else effect = movement or transfer (one alone is to or from someone else: stored as movement)
            opt names a Holding (a dividend, interest or tax)
                alt that Holding is archived by its user (FR-5.8)
                    TM-->>API: Conflict
                    API-->>User: 409 holding.archived — a closed position takes no events (OQ-171)
                end
            end
            TM->>AA: applyMovement(accountId, {amount, currency, date})
            AA-->>TM: new balance
        else effect = bill_payment
            Note over TM: the payer's gates passed — now the card's own Account (OQ-219)
            TM->>AZ: canRecord(actor, the card's accountId)?
            alt the actor may not record on the card's Account
                AZ-->>TM: not visible
                TM-->>API: NotFound
                API-->>User: 404 credit_card.not_found
            else the card's Account is archived
                TM-->>API: Conflict
                API-->>User: 409 credit_card.account_archived
            else the card's Account is in another currency
                TM-->>API: ValidationError
                API-->>User: 422 transaction.currency_mismatch {accountCurrency}
            else allowed
                TM->>AA: applyMovement(accountId, {-amount, currency, date})
                AA-->>TM: account debited (OQ-15)
                TM->>CCA: applyMovement(cardId, {-amount, currency, date})
                CCA-->>TM: outstanding balance reduced
            end
        else effect = investment_trade
            Note over TM: quantityDelta and costBasisDelta computed here (FR-3.8):
a buy adds what was paid, a sell or redemption removes its average cost (OQ-171),
a sell or redemption names its Holding by holdingId (OQ-201)
            TM->>AA: applyMovement(accountId, {−amount for a buy, +amount for a sell or redemption, currency, date})
            AA-->>TM: cash moved (OQ-201 — history only when dated before the starting balance, OQ-169)
            opt kind = investment buy
                alt assetType is one of FR-11.1's fixed-term instruments
                    TM->>IHA: insert(holding) — a new Holding, always (OQ-50)
                    IHA-->>TM: new Holding
                else market-priced
                    TM->>IHA: findByTicker(accountId, assetType, ticker)
                    IHA-->>TM: the Holding, archived ones included (OQ-171)
                    opt none
                        TM->>IHA: insert(holding) (auto-create, FR-5.1/OQ-41)
                        IHA-->>TM: new Holding
                    end
                end
            end
            TM->>IHA: applyTrade(holdingId, {quantityDelta, costBasisDelta})
            IHA-->>TM: {quantity, costBasis}
            Note over IHA: refused below zero: insufficient_quantity / cost_basis_below_zero
            opt market-priced assetType (OQ-51)
                TM->>VSA: insertAutomatic(holdingId, transactionId, date)
                TM->>ATA: listTrades(holdingId)
                TM->>VSA: list(holdingId)
                Note over TM: restate the Holding's history in order: a typed value sets it, a buy adds
its amount, a sell takes its amount off (never below 0), sold out is 0 — the money,
not the units (OQ-222, holdingValues.ts)
                TM->>VSA: updateAutomatic(each trade whose value or date moved)
                Note over VSA: source = transaction — changes only with its transaction (OQ-56)
            end
            alt quantity = 0 (full sell or redemption)
                TM->>IHA: archive(holdingId, by = system)
                Note over IHA: auto-archive (FR-5.8, OQ-72) — undone if this
transaction is later edited or deleted (OQ-80)
            else a buy into a system-archived Holding
                TM->>IHA: unarchiveIfSystemArchived(holdingId)
                Note over IHA: the re-buy reopens the position (OQ-171)
            end
            opt assetType is a defined FR-11.1 instrument type
                Note over TM,IPE: dueDate, rate and the debenture incentivised flag are
user-supplied — not derivable from assetType alone (OQ-50, OQ-79)
                TM->>IPE: schedule(householdId, {assetType, incentivised, date, amount, dueDate, rate, interestPayment})
                Note over IPE: reads the latest index value itself for an index-linked rate — amounts are projections,
BR business days (252), US actual/365, at maturity interest + tax + redemption (OQ-201)
                alt the index has no value yet (OQ-205)
                    IPE-->>TM: {ok: false, reason: index_missing, indexCode}
                    TM-->>API: refused — nothing kept
                    API-->>User: 422 index_rate.missing {indexCode}
                else
                    IPE-->>TM: entries — tax rules coded per type, US = none estimated
                    TM->>IHA: applySchedule(holdingId, schedule)
                    IHA-->>TM: schedule stored (replace-all, refused once an entry posted, OQ-171)
                end
            end
            opt allocations provided (OQ-54, OQ-168)
                TM->>GA: findByIds(householdId, goalIds)
                GA-->>TM: the named Goals
                TM->>GA: listAllocations({holdingId})
                GA-->>TM: the Holding's current split
                alt a named Goal isn't visible to the actor
                    TM-->>API: NotFound
                    API-->>User: 404 goal.not_found
                else a named Goal is completed (FR-8.9)
                    TM-->>API: Conflict
                    API-->>User: 409 goal.completed
                else the purchase's own split passes 100% (FR-8.3)
                    TM-->>API: Conflict
                    API-->>User: 409 goal.allocation_exceeds_100 {current, requested}
                else
                    Note over TM: the split applies to the purchase: re-weight the existing split by value
(the Holding's value before the buy, its latest snapshot — OQ-222) plus the purchase's shares —
largest remainder, a share rounding to 0% dropped, completed Goals re-weighted too
                    TM->>GA: replaceAllocations(householdId, holdingId, shares)
                    GA-->>TM: replaced
                end
            end
        end
        TM->>BA: accrueIfClaimed({accountScope, currency, categoryId, subcategoryId, ±amount, date})
        BA-->>TM: spend updated (any kind may carry a category, money in subtracts — OQ-201)
        TM->>ATA: insert({…transaction, effect, trade: {quantityDelta, costBasisDelta}, createdByUserId})
        alt an Account, Holding, card or user it names was deleted meanwhile
            ATA-->>TM: {ok: false, reason: reference_not_found}
            Note over TM: refuse — the unit of work rolls every write above back (OQ-107)
            TM-->>API: Conflict
            API-->>User: 409 transaction.changed (retry gets the precise 404)
        else recorded
            ATA-->>TM: {ok: true, transaction}
            TM-->>LOG: logActivity(correlationId, actor, "RecordTransaction")
            TM-->>LOG: recordAudit(actor, "Transaction", transactionId)
            TM-->>API: transaction
            API-->>User: 201 Created {transaction}
        end
    end
  • This is the same pipeline as the VBD backend doc's §5 sequence diagram (Record an Account Transaction), reused here rather than redrawn from scratch — the Card Transaction branch and the HTTP/route layer are the only genuinely new detail added.
  • Two separate ledgers, one bridge: Card Transactions and Account Transactions are two distinct tables with no FK between them (OQ-16) — the only bridge is a Credit Card Bill Payment, an Account Transaction carrying a credit_card_id.
  • No NotificationDeliveryUtility call anywhere in this flow — FR-4.4's threshold alert was removed (OQ-52, Round 8).
  • Oct 2026 review: automatic Valuation Snapshot is explicit (source = transaction, OQ-56); a full sell/redemption auto-archives the Holding (OQ-72); investment kinds rejected on credit-card-only accounts (OQ-76); the Engine call carries the debenture flag and returns coded taxes (OQ-79); error responses use OQ-83 codes. No recurring option (OQ-55).
  • Re-buying a zeroed Holding (OQ-171): a market-priced buy reopens the system-archived Holding of its ticker. Its old Goal split is worth 0 at quantity 0, so the buy's re-weighting (OQ-168) drops every old share — the new buy's split is the only one left; undoing the zeroing sell instead restores the old split intact.
  • Goal allocation can be bundled here (OQ-54, added after the API coverage audit): one call in, no separate PATCH /households/:householdId/holdings/{holdingId} follow-up required — same 100%-cap rejection as that endpoint's own allocation edit (FR-8.3), just reachable from this pipeline too. Direction of control is unchanged (OQ-48): still written from the Holding's side, never the Goal's.
  • The buy's split is the purchase's (OQ-168): buying into a Holding that already funds Goals re-weights its whole split by value — its value before the buy, the latest snapshot (amended in OQ-222) — so every Goal keeps exactly its money (900 at Retirement 60% / Trip 20%, plus 100 for Car 100% → 54% / 18% / 10%). One replace-all write, so a refusal never leaves half a split behind.
  • Slice 3 readiness (OQ-201): every trade moves the account's cash (a buy debits it, a sell or redemption credits it); sell, redemption, dividend, interest and tax name their Holding by holdingId; a credit-card-only account takes no Account Transaction — another account pays its cards' bills (OQ-219); any kind may carry a category and money in reduces the claiming Budget's spend; the trade's deltas and the recorder are stored with the transaction. A transfer recorded alone is to or from someone else and is stored as movement, so it counts; one between the household's own accounts (toAccountId) records both sides, linked, with effect = transfer (OQ-222, reversing D2).
  • Bill payments (OQ-219): any open Account but a credit-card-only one pays a card in its currency. The payer's gates come first, then the card's own Account: one the actor may edit (404 credit_card.not_found), open (409 credit_card.account_archived) and in the payment's currency.
  • All writes share one database transaction (UnitOfWorkUtility) — any refusal rolls every one back.
