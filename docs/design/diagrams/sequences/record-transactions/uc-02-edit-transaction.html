PATCH /households/:householdId/transactions/:transactionId — FR-3.2. Works for either an Account or Card Transaction, the same unified entry point (OQ-39). Real complexity: every downstream effect must be reversed, then reapplied with the new values.

  • PR #145 review: a trade's change restates its Holding's automatic snapshots from the whole history (listTrades, list, then updateAutomatic where a value or date moved), so later trades' values follow it; a pair's two sides are written in Account-id order.
  • OQ-222: either side of a transfer between own accounts edits both as one — amount is what was sent, receivedAmount what arrived across currencies, no category; the caller must be able to edit both accounts (403 otherwise).
  • Reverse-then-reapply, all inside one transaction — the same complexity flagged in 02-data-model.md's Transactions domain, now traced call-by-call. If the edit only changes the date to a different month, the reverse/reapply pair is what actually drives the backdated-balance cascade documented in the Accounts domain (shift every subsequent month's opening/ending balance by one delta).
  • CardTransactionAccessor.update path (not shown, same shape) applies when the target was a Card Transaction instead of an Account Transaction.
  • Oct 2026 review: the automatic snapshot follows the edit (the value before the trade, its own snapshot left out, ± the new amount — OQ-222); the Holding is re-archived or un-archived to match the new quantity (OQ-72, OQ-80).
  • OQ-201: the trade's cash leg is undone and redone with it; a buy or sell may change its quantity, a fixed-term buy its rate, due date, interest payment and debenture flag — and its schedule is recomputed while no entry has posted. Once one has, its amount, date and terms are locked (409). The Budget accrual is undone and redone with its sign (money in subtracts). Transactions the schedule posted edit like any other.
  • OQ-219: a bill payment's card may sit on another Account than its payer, so editing or deleting it decides on both: the card's Account must still be one the actor may edit (404 credit_card.not_found) and open (409 credit_card.account_archived).
