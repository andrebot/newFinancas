  • Route under /reports by stakeholder decision (sequence review Q1): month overview is a report, and reports expand in v2 (ad-hoc types, CSV export).
  • One currency is shown at a time in the UI ($/R$ switch) — the response carries every currency, never summed (FR-2.2).
  • OQ-201: In/Out count card charges and refunds and investment buys and sells, leave out transfers and bill payments; a card shows its outstanding balance at the month's end; each Budget item carries its id, name, currency and visibility, and one with no period opened that month shows the target in effect and zero spend; Goals come per currency — the frontend shows the Goal's own currency and a note when others fund it. Archived Accounts are left out.
  • OQ-206: the strip is January–December of the selected year; a fixed-term Holding's value at the month's end is its trades' cost basis by then; a Goal's funding in an Account the viewer can't see counts with no id or name.
  • See FR-6.11, FR-6.6, FR-4.3, FR-8.5, OQ-74, OQ-206.
