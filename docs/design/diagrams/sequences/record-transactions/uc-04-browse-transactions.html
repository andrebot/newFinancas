Browse & Filter Transactions

GET /households/:householdId/transactions?kinds=&accountIds=&creditCardIds=&categoryIds=&holdingId=&from=&to=&cursor= — FR-3.12/OQ-61. Both ledgers merged, newest first, cursor-paginated. Owned by TransactionManager (plain read of its own ledger — read-ownership rule).

    API-->>User: 200 OK
  • Not scoped to the period bar (OQ-61): any date range, including none.
  • Filtering by holdingId returns the investment transactions linked to it — the holding row's "See in Transactions" link.
  • Filters (OQ-201): combined with AND; accounts and cards are one picker (a row matches when its account or card is selected, a card's rows also when its Account is — OQ-204; none = all); a Category includes its Subcategories.
  • No logging/audit — a plain read (FR-7.1 audits mutations only).
  • See FR-3.12, OQ-61; read-ownership rule, VBD §3.
