Leave Household

DELETE /households/:householdId/members/:userId (userId = the caller) — FR-1.11/FR-1.18. Self-service, no authorization check. If the leaving member is the Owner, the longest-tenured member takes over first, or the household dissolves when nobody else is left.

  • All branches return the same 204, but the decisions are IdentityManager's (OQ-107): who succeeds, and whether the household dissolves. HouseholdAccessor applies each step as a guarded write and says stale when the facts changed meanwhile — the whole decision then runs again from fresh facts, up to three times (households.leaveAttempts), before a 409. dissolve is guarded too (OQ-117): someone who joined a moment ago is never deleted with the household.
  • Changed (task 136, OQ-240): leaving takes the caller's finances in the household with them — the same erasure as Delete User, scoped to this household (02-data-model.md's trigger equivalence). It commits in one transaction with each attempt at departing: a stale attempt rolls its erasure back, so a 409 leaves the caller a member with nothing erased.
  • This is the richest of the three "membership ends" flows (Remove Member, Leave, and — via FR-1.17 — Delete User) precisely because it's the one place FR-1.18's succession rule is directly, voluntarily triggered rather than a side effect of account deletion.
