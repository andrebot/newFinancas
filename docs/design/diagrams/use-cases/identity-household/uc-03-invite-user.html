Changed in the Oct 2026 review: the invitee is notified through the in-app notification inbox (FR-6.5, OQ-68) — v1 has no email channel. The notification carries who invited them (FR-1.10).

Scope boundary: this use case ends once a pending invitation exists and its notice has been sent. The invitee's side — accepting or declining — is a separate use case (UC-4/UC-5, FR-1.10), triggered from an invitation link/notice rather than from Household Settings.

Existing-user-only constraint (FR-1.19 / OQ-26): there is no way to invite someone who isn't already a registered user — no invite-triggers-signup flow in this design. An invite to an email with no matching account is rejected at this step.
