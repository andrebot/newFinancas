UC-10: Transfer Ownership

Use case 10 of 21 in the Identity & Household set (docs/requirements/01-core-use-cases.md, CUC-1). Traces to FR-1.20. Owner-initiated, voluntary hand-off while remaining a household member — see UC-8 (Leave) and UC-11 (Delete Account) for the auto-succession path (FR-1.18) that applies when the Owner departs entirely. High-level activity view only — no component names or API details yet.

Atomicity is the invariant (FR-1.20 / OQ-28): the promotion and demotion are one write, never two — a household must never be observably at zero or two Owners, even momentarily. Same category of invariant as FR-1.18's succession check.

Outgoing Owner lands on Admin — not a choice offered in the UI for v1.
