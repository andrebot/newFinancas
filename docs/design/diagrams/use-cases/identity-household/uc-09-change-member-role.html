UC-9: Change a Member's Role

Use case 9 of 21 in the Identity & Household set (docs/requirements/01-core-use-cases.md, CUC-1). Traces to FR-1.12. Owner/Admin acting on someone else, scoped to Admin/Member/Viewer only — see UC-10 for the dedicated Owner-role transfer. High-level activity view only — no component names or API details yet.

Owner role is out of scope here (FR-1.12): this action can never assign or remove the Owner role — attempting to do so is rejected at the same step that verifies the target isn't already the Owner. The Owner role only ever moves via Transfer Ownership (UC-10, FR-1.20) or the auto-succession triggered by Leave (UC-8) / Delete Account (UC-11).
