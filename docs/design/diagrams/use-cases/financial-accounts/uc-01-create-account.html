Account types (OQ-76, Oct 2026 review): four — checking, savings, credit card only, investment. Investments can later live in any of them except credit card only. Also the mandatory first step of the guided setup for every new household (FR-1.22).

Type and currency lock in here (OQ-32): both are immutable after this point. Visibility (personal/shared) stays editable later (FR-2.3/OQ-33).

Credit cards, two entry points: optionally inline here at creation, or later via Edit Account (UC-4, Attach Credit Card) — same underlying capability, two different triggers.
