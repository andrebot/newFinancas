Overlap check (FR-4.7): a Category/Subcategory can be claimed by at most one Budget per scope/period — claiming a whole Category reserves all its Subcategories too.

Budget vs. Budget Period (FR-4.9): creating a Budget also immediately locks in the current month's Budget Period — this is what keeps future edits from silently rewriting past history.

Not covered here: viewing actual-vs-target status belongs to CUC-8 (it's a Dashboard Widget, FR-6.7).
