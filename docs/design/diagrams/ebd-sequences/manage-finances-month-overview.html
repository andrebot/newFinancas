    EXP->>API: GET /households/:householdId/reports/month-overview?year=&month= (today's month)

    EXP-->>FLOW: period, report (loading / failed / loaded), accounts

    FLOW-->>CS: render In / Out / Total (R$ the first time, then the remembered pick)

    alt pick another month or year
        User->>PB: select
        PB->>FLOW: emit periodChanged
        FLOW->>EXP: periodChanged(year, month)
        EXP->>API: GET /households/:householdId/reports/month-overview?year=&month=
    else switch currency
        User->>CS: $ or R$
        CS->>FLOW: emit currencyChanged (local — remembered in this browser, no request)
    else the month fails
        EXP-->>FLOW: failed
        FLOW-->>RL: banner "Couldn't load Month/Year" + Try again, in the rails' place
        User->>FLOW: Try again
        FLOW->>EXP: retry
        EXP->>API: GET /households/:householdId/reports/month-overview?year=&month=
    end
  • FormattingUtility formats every amount in its own currency; nothing is summed across currencies.
  • OQ-201: a Goal funded in other currencies shows its own currency's progress and a note pointing to Goals (the exchange rate lives there); a card shows its outstanding at the month's end.
  • OQ-221: the Experience holds the month (its query's argument) and refetches it after a record, edit or delete; the Flow keeps only the $ / R$ switch. Its two regions (bar, rails) are placed by the Experience's page grid around Browse's ledger. A failed month never shows the previous month's figures.
